## What we sell

CLYMEX AI is a **climate risk intelligence platform**, not just a weather map.
It aims to turn Earth observation, meteorology, spatial exposure and IoT sensor data into local, explainable intelligence for decisions about floods, drought and water scarcity.

**Buyer segments (hypotheses to validate):**
1. Public institutions and water basin managers (institutional licences / annual subscriptions).
2. Agricultural operations, insurers and infrastructure operators (portfolio risk monitoring).
3. NGOs and resilience projects (programme licences and analytical reports).
4. Integrators and researchers (developer/data API after calibration and validation).

No customers, paid pilots, accuracy, savings or impact metrics are claimed until independently evidenced.

## Differentiation and cross-border narrative

- Mozambique-first: administrative geography powered by GeoMoz, localized district and provincial views.
- Multi-hazard: flooding, agricultural drought and water stress in one decision interface.
- Explainability: expose source, acquisition date, inferred or observed mode, confidence and drivers.
- China–Lusophone bridge: seek partnerships with Chinese IoT manufacturers and remote-sensing/AI technology providers, research organisations in Macau/Greater Bay Area, and eventual deployment in Portuguese-speaking markets.
- Scale-out concept: later expand to Southern Africa, with basin-based rather than country-only geographies.

The China/GBA pathway is **a proposed commercial partnership**, not an existing contract.

## Live demo scenario (three minutes)

1. **Problem (30 s):** Show how flood and drought conditions can harm communities and infrastructure. Avoid unverified numerical claims.
2. **Geography (30 s):** Show Mozambique's provincial GeoMoz boundaries and the eight pilot districts. Explain how Chókwè belongs to the Limpopo basin, not the Incomati.
3. **Risk insight (45 s):** Switch between Flood, Drought and Water Stress. Explain risk scores and how missing data is treated. Distinguish DEMO/ESTIMATED/OBSERVED.
4. **Exposure (30 s):** Show estimated exposed buildings/population. Say these are indicative estimates, not a validated census or hazard intersection.
5. **Action (30 s):** Show the SitRep workflow as a demonstrator and how institutions could use traceable, validated outputs after a pilot.
6. **Business (15 s):** Proposed subscription, pilot buyers and partnerships.

**Never demonstrate synthetic flood extents, legacy static satellite metrics or map base imagery as fresh measured flood detections.**

## Risk of misleading prototype indicators

GeoMoz supplies administrative **boundaries**, not real-time flood forecasts.
The current risk engine still combines real meteorological requests with
demonstration/heuristic parameters. A current risk score must be treated as
**indicative/estimated**, not an official hazard warning. Government emergency
instructions and early warnings take precedence.
